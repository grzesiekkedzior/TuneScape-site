TuneScape 6.0.0 · Now available

Thousands of stations.
One clean player.

A free and open-source internet radio player for Windows. Browse stations from around the world, save favorites, record streams and control playback without leaving the system tray.

Download for Windows View source

Free forever · No ads · No accounts · No telemetry

Windows desktop app Qt 6 + C++ native build GPLv3 open source Radio Browser powered
Designed for everyday listening

Everything you need.
Nothing in the way.

TuneScape keeps discovery, playback and everyday controls in one focused desktop application.

01

Worldwide station browser

Explore thousands of community-maintained stations with a clean, efficient list view powered by radio-browser.info.

02

Search and country browsing

Find a station by name or narrow the list by country when you want something local or completely new.

03

Favorites and station details

Keep your regular stations close, inspect stream details and open a broadcaster’s homepage directly.

04

Live cover art and metadata

See the current track, cover artwork and available artist information while the station is playing.

05

System tray controls

Pause playback, switch station lists and keep the Now Playing panel visible without reopening the main window.

06

Stream recording

Record the current radio stream locally and monitor the saved data while the recording is in progress.

07

Top, popular and new stations

Jump quickly into curated station lists and discover what Radio Browser listeners are enjoying.

08

Five interface themes

Choose System, Light, Dark, Classic or Cyberpunk and make the player feel at home on your desktop.

  • Open curated station lists from the tray

Major release
6.0.0

Focused on the way
you actually listen.

Version 6 is a cleaner, more focused TuneScape. Legacy secondary modes have been removed so development can concentrate on the main Radio Browser experience.
